What this is not. This is a text comparison, not a code review and not a substitute for a human checker. Sheets are matched by sheet number, so a renumbered sheet shows up as one removed plus one added. Scanned pages with no text layer cannot be compared. Always confirm against the sheets themselves before you act on a result.
